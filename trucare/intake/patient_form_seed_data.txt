SEED BLUEPRINT FOR DYNAMIC CONDITION FORM
Source: patient_form.html
Target models: Condition, ChoiceSet, Choice, ConditionQuestion

FORMAT NOTES
- condition_code must fit max_length=30 in Condition.code.
- question_type must be one of: text, yes_no, number, date, single_choice, multiple_choice.
- For yes_no questions, set question_type=yes_no and leave choice_set empty.
- For single_choice/multiple_choice, attach the listed choice_set.
- The top "condition request" selects should map to PatientCondition.requested (boolean).
